Simple Media App

Everything runs locally on your machine. Reads .mov, .mp4 and more; saves MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM.

Drag the handles to choose the part to keep. Click the strip to move the playhead.
Your browser can't preview this video, so enter the times by hand. Exporting still works. Leave both empty to keep the whole video (e.g. to only convert it).
Fast mode copies the streams only if they fit the chosen format. If not, the video is re-encoded.
